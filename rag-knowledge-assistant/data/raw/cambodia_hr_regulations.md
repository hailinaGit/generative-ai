# Cambodia Labor Law & Internal Regulations Compliance Guide
Document ID: KHM-LABOR-REG-2026

## 1. Employment & Working Conditions
- **Applicability:** For companies with 8 or more employees, having a formal set of Internal Regulations (IR) is a legal requirement under the Cambodian Labor Law, and it must be written in Khmer and approved by the Ministry of Labour and Vocational Training (MLVT).
- **Working Hours:** Standard working hours are capped at 8 hours per day or 48 hours per week maximum.
- **Overtime Regulations:** Overtime requires prior approval, is capped at 2 hours per day, and must be compensated at:
  - 150% for daytime work.
  - 200% for nighttime work or Sundays.
  - 250% for official public holidays.
- **Probationary Period:** Limited to a maximum of 3 months for regular employees, 2 months for specialized workers, and 1 month for non-skilled workers.

## 2. Leave & Time-Off Policies
- **Annual Leave:** Standard workers receive 18 days per year, increasing by 1 additional day for every 3 years of continuous service.
- **Sick Leave:** Up to 6 months of sick leave certified by an approved doctor (typically paid at 100% for the 1st month, 60% for the 2nd/3rd month, and unpaid after).
- **Maternity Leave:** 90 days of leave with half-pay (50% of salary plus benefits) for female employees with at least one year of seniority, in accordance with Articles 182 and 183 of the Labor Law.
- **Special Leave:** Up to 7 days per year for immediate family events (marriage, births, funerals), which can be deducted from annual leave if available.

## 3. Compensation & Benefits
- **Salary Payment:** Paid at least twice a month (the first payment is a 50% advance, and the second is the balance payment).
- **Seniority Pay:** Mandatory for Undetermined Duration Contracts (UDC). Paid bi-annually (7.5 days of wages/benefits in June, and 7.5 days in December).
- **NSSF Contributions:** Mandatory employer and employee contributions to the National Social Security Fund for Occupational Risk, Healthcare, and Pension.

## 4. Workplace Discipline & Code of Conduct
- **Disciplinary Scale:** Follows the progressive discipline pathway allowed by law: Verbal warning $\rightarrow$ Written warning $\rightarrow$ Misconduct suspension (max 7 days) $\rightarrow$ Termination.
- **Serious Misconduct:** Immediate termination without notice or indemnity for offenses such as theft, embezzlement, violent behavior, or exposing trade secrets.
- **Harassment Policy:** Zero-tolerance clauses covering sexual harassment and gender-based discrimination compliant with MLVT directives.
