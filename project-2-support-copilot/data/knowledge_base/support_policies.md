# Official Customer Support Policy Handbook
Document ID: POL-SUPPORT-2026-V1
Last Updated: November 2026

---

## 1. Refund & Billing Policy
- **Standard Refund Window:** Customers may request a full refund within 14 calendar days of purchase for unused digital services or unopened physical items.
- **Refund Processing Limits:**
  - Frontline support agents are authorized to issue direct refunds up to **$100.00 USD**.
  - Any refund request exceeding **$100.00 USD**, or any request involving bank chargeback threats or legal dispute claims, must be flagged for **Tier-2 Supervisor Escalation**. Frontline agents and AI copilots MUST NOT promise refunds above $100 without supervisor approval.
- **Processing Time:** Approved refunds take 3 to 5 business days to post back to the original payment method.
- **Non-Refundable Items:** Custom digital enterprise licenses, consumable in-game items, and shipping postage fees after carrier dispatch are strictly non-refundable.

---

## 2. Account Security & Access Recovery
- **Password Reset:** Customers who forgot their password must use the automated self-service portal at `app.company.com/forgot-password`. Support agents must never ask for or transmit plaintext passwords.
- **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) Lockouts:**
  - If a user loses their authenticator device or phone number, identity verification is required.
  - The customer must provide: (1) Last 4 digits of the payment method on file, (2) Invoice number of their most recent transaction, and (3) Associated account registration email.
  - Once verified, agents issue a one-time emergency 2FA reset link valid for 15 minutes.
- **Compromised Account Protocol:** If suspicious activity or unauthorized logins are reported, the agent must immediately freeze the account session and route to the Security Fraud Team.

---

## 3. Shipping, Delivery & Missing Orders
- **Delivery Timeframes:**
  - Standard Domestic Shipping: 3 to 5 business days.
  - Express Priority Shipping: 1 to 2 business days.
  - International Air Mail: 7 to 14 business days.
- **Delayed & In-Transit Packages:** If tracking has not updated for more than 48 hours, support will file a trace with the delivery carrier.
- **Lost or Damaged in Transit:**
  - An order is declared lost if no delivery scan occurs after 7 business days past the estimated delivery date.
  - In cases of lost items, customers are entitled to a **free replacement reshipment** or a **full store credit voucher**.

---

## 4. Subscription Cancellation & Downgrades
- **Self-Service Cancellation:** Subscriptions can be canceled at any time from `Settings > Billing > Cancel Plan`.
- **Cancellation Effective Date:** Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. Customers retain full access to paid features until the expiration date.
- **Prorated Credits:** Mid-month cancellations do not receive partial cash refunds; rather, remaining days remain active or can be converted to account credits upon request.
- **Data Retention Post-Cancellation:** Account data and files are preserved for 60 days following plan cancellation before permanent deletion. Customers may export their data via `Settings > Data Export` at any time.

---

## 5. Tone Guidelines & Safety Controls
- **Tone & Persona:** Empathetic, calm, solution-oriented, and professional. Acknowledge customer frustration first before explaining policy.
- **Forbidden Commitments:** Never guarantee immediate delivery times, never promise financial compensation not approved in the handbook, and never argue with an angry customer.
- **Fallback Trigger:** If a customer's inquiry falls completely outside of these policies (e.g., questions about competitor products, legal advice, or unrelated third-party software), decline politely:
  > *"I cannot find this information in our approved support policies. I have routed your ticket to a specialist for review."*
